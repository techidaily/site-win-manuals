## DEX 3.20.6 Recap:

  
**KJs Rejoice:** No more song/singer duplicates in your history. Plus, swiftly find all songs by your favorite singer with a simple search query. And, we took care of that pesky bug that didn’t let you favorite the first track in the catalog/search results.

**Controller Compatibility Boost:** Enhanced mappings for controllers including Pioneer DDJ-1000, Pioneer DDJ-1000 SRT, Numark DJ2GO2 Touch, and Numark MixTrack Platinum FX.

**Cleaner Browser Experience:** Third-party streaming services will now be collapsed by default.

**Beatport/Beatsource Bliss:** Introducing guest mode improvements and dedicated icons to spruce up your interface.

**Advanced Audio Configurations:** The setAudioDevice() function now comes with (optional) advanced audio routing settings. This is for the tech-savvy DJs looking for an edge.
